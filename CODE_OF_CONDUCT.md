# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We expect all participants to uphold these standards.

---

## Our Standards

### ✅ DO

- Be respectful and inclusive
- Listen to different viewpoints
- Be patient and helpful
- Focus on constructive feedback
- Assume good intentions
- Report inappropriate behavior

### ❌ DON'T

- Use offensive language or personal attacks
- Discriminate based on identity or background
- Engage in harassment or bullying
- Share others' private information
- Spam or self-promote excessively
- Violate intellectual property rights

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- GitHub discussions and issues
- Pull request comments
- Commit messages
- Documentation
- Any project-related communication

---

## Enforcement

Violations will be handled promptly and fairly:

1. **First Offense:** Private warning
2. **Second Offense:** Public notice and temporary block
3. **Repeated Violations:** Permanent ban from community

---

## Reporting

Report violations to: **manthandsoni@gmail.com**

Include:
- Description of behavior
- Context and impact
- Desired resolution
